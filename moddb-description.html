Lampwright

A belt that lights your way, so both hands stay free.

Vintage Story only lights you from what you are holding. Carry a torch and you give up a hand; carry a lantern and you give up the offhand. Pick up a two-handed weapon, a bow, or a shield and you walk blind.

Lampwright's Belt is a tinker's belt with four temporal gears set into the leather, feeding a small brass lamp at the hip. Wear it and it lights the ground around you at light level 17 — brighter than a torch, a shade under a lantern — from the waist slot, which costs you nothing you were using. It never burns down and never needs filling.

Built for anyone running heavy lighting or path-tracing shaders, where the world is genuinely dark and a free hand matters.

Crafting

A ring in the 3x3 grid, around any belt you already own:

  • 4x temporal gear
  • 4x flax twine
  • 1x any waist belt, in the centre

The gears go in the four corners and the twine in the four edges. Temporal gears stack to one, so they have to occupy four separate cells — that is why the recipe is shaped this way rather than asking for a stack of four.

That gives you the temporal belt, which glows cyan. The other five colours come out of a barrel.

Six Colours

Seal the belt in a barrel with 1 litre of dye for 4 hours, the same way you dye cloth and daub:

  • Temporal — cyan, the colour it is crafted at
  • Orange — exactly a lit torch
  • Red
  • Yellow — warm, close to a gas lamp
  • Blue — cool sky blue
  • White — neutral, made from borax

Any colour re-dyes straight to any other, so you never have to go back through temporal first. Putting a temporal gear in the barrel instead of dye fits the original lens back and returns it to cyan.

The light the belt casts always matches the lens you can see on the model, and the glow lands on you as well as on the ground.

Wear And Repair

The belt is ordinary clothing: it wears down as you use it, gives 0.3 °C of warmth, and takes a sewing kit (+75%), cloth or linen (+50%) or flax twine (+10%) to put back — pick the material up on your cursor and click it onto the belt.

Condition only scales the warmth. A belt worn down to its last threads still lights at full strength.

How The Light Works

The belt is a moving point light, the same kind the game already gives a held torch. Nothing is baked into chunk lighting, so there is no relight cost when you walk and no lingering bright patches behind you.

It does not replace what is in your hands — a torch and the belt stack rather than cancelling each other, so you can still light a tunnel ahead while the belt covers your feet.

Two things worth knowing before you install:

  • Dynamic lights have to be on. If your client has Max Dynamic Lights set to 0, you will see no glow at all. That is a client graphics setting the mod cannot override. The default is 10.
  • Other players see it too, provided the server has the mod. The light is published by the server and drawn by each client, so a lit belt is visible across a multiplayer world rather than only to the wearer.

Compatibility

Required on both client and server. This mod adds an item, and the server is what tells every client that a belt is lit, so a client-only install will not work on a multiplayer server — it has to be installed on the server as well.

No dependencies. Built and tested against Vintage Story 1.22.7. The belt is an ordinary item in an ordinary clothing slot, so adding or removing the mod does not disturb a world beyond the belts themselves.
